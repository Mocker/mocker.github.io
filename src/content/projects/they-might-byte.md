# They Might Byte

They Might Byte is an independent product venture aimed at developer culture: apparel and digital goods with a bite. It started as a monster pet game built on a Global Game Jam 2022 entry and is growing into a broader brand for people who write code — planned as high-margin, automated print-on-demand fulfillment rather than held inventory.
